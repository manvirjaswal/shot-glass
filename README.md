# Shot Glass

A VR rhythm shooter played with your bare hands. Glass discs light up on the beat; point a finger, flick your hand up like
a pistol's kick, and they shatter. A fuse runs from disc to disc to show the order. Two hands, two colours: red is your
left, blue your right.

**Play:** open https://manvirjaswal.github.io/shot-glass/ in the Meta Quest Browser, put the controllers down, and press
Enter VR.

- Hands only, from the first menu to the results. Controllers also work.
- Made to be played seated: everything is in front of you, and nothing asks you to stand, step or lean.
- A three-minute tutorial, four songs, six difficulties from Easy to Master.

## Controls (hand tracking)

| | |
|---|---|
| Shoot | Point at a disc and flick your hand up |
| Press a button | Point at it and pinch (or flick) |
| Pause | Pinch and hold for half a second during a song |
